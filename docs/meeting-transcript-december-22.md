## Key Discussion Points

### Intake Form Walkthrough @ 4:10

Sean walks through the intake form he has designed, which allows patients to input their personal information, symptoms, and medical history. Liza and Susan provide feedback on how to improve the form.

**Key Feedback:**
- Use "symptoms" instead of "pain" (psychological consideration - don't put pain in patient's mind)
- Add options to describe the onset and behavior of the symptoms
- Include screening questions for mental health (PHQ-2, PHQ-4, GADS) and social drivers of health
- Onset date should allow "best guess" or approximate dates
- Include SINS framework (Symptoms, Irritability, Nature, Severity)
- Red flags and yellow flags questions
- Group medical history by body systems (respiratory, cardiovascular, etc.)
- Include medications list
- Pre-visit instructions: ID, insurance card, prescription, medications list, clothing requirements

**Action Items:**
- Revise intake: symptoms-first, onset 'best guess', SINS, red/yellow flags, PHQ-2/PHQ-4/GADS, meds, free text
- Email Sean red/yellow flag Qs + SINS definitions
- Add pre-visit instructions to intake/booking: ID, insurance, script, meds, clothing

### Objective Assessment and Documentation @ 22:03

Liza demonstrates the clinic's current software for documenting the physical therapy evaluation and treatment. This includes sections for vitals, range of motion, muscle testing, special tests, functional testing, and the treatment plan.

**Key Insights:**
- Vitals: blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation
- Palpation and ocular inspection
- Muscle testing, range of motion (AROM and PROM)
- Dermatomes, myotomes, sensory testing, reflex testing
- Functional activity/functional testing
- Special tests
- Current functional limitations
- Medical history (grouped by systems)
- Previous surgeries
- Assessment and problem list
- Treatment plan with goals
- Billing codes and units

**Workflow Challenges:**
- Difficult to type while with patient (hands-on work)
- Often complete documentation after hours
- Need for voice dictation/recording capability
- Visual prompts/guides would be helpful

**Action Items:**
- Research recording/privacy/HIPAA; if feasible add intake consent + mobile containerization + on-screen transcription + visual prompts
- Email Sean sample note template (PDF)

### Billing and Coding Considerations @ 1:48:46

The discussion covers the complexities of billing and coding for physical therapy services, including the need to justify the modalities used and the time spent on each component of the evaluation and treatment.

**Key Points:**
- Need to justify modalities based on goals
- Time tracking for each component
- Billing codes: 97110 (therapeutic exercise), 97112 (neuromuscular re-education), 97130 (therapeutic activities)
- Units calculation based on time
- Insurance-specific billing requirements
- Shortcuts/workarounds created due to complexity
- Document locking after completion
- Ability to edit locked documents with addendum
- Management system to review other PTs' documents

**Challenges:**
- Complex billing process requiring shortcuts
- Need for accurate time tracking
- Insurance-specific requirements
- Document accuracy critical for insurance audits
